# Product Guidelines

## Brand Voice & Tone
YAKAP communicates with a balanced voice that adapts to the user's context:
*   **Empathetic & Gentle:** The primary tone for user-facing interactions, mood tracking, and support features. We use comforting language and "emotion-first" microcopy (e.g., "We're here for you").
*   **Professional & Clinical:** Used for medical records, appointment scheduling, and professional-facing tools to ensure clarity and trust.
*   **Encouraging & Upbeat:** Applied to milestones, progress tracking, and wellness exercises to motivate users.

## Visual Identity
The visual language combines **Calm & Minimalist** elements with **Warm & Organic** touches to create a safe, healing environment.
*   **Color Palette:** Soft blues and greens for tranquility, complemented by warm earthy tones (browns, warm yellows) to add comfort.
*   **Shapes:** Rounded corners and soft edges to reduce visual stress.
*   **Imagery:** Gentle, abstract, or nature-inspired illustrations.
*   **Dark Mode:** Fully supported to reduce eye strain, especially for evening usage.

## User Experience (UX) Principles
1.  **Compassionate & Human-Centered:** Every interaction should feel safe and supportive.
2.  **Privacy & Security:** Visual indicators (e.g., lock icons, distinct colors) must clearly show when data is encrypted or private.
3.  **Low Cognitive Load:** Interfaces are simplified to avoid overwhelming users, particularly those in distress.
4.  **Accessibility First:** High contrast options, large touch targets, and full screen reader support are mandatory.

## UI Guidelines
*   **Navigation:**
    *   **Patients/Users:** Bottom navigation bar for easy mobile access.
    *   **Professionals/Admins:** Side drawers or tabs to handle complex management tasks.
*   **Responsiveness:** Layouts must be built with Jetpack Compose to adapt seamlessly between phones and tablets.
*   **Animation:** Use gentle, slow-moving animations for breathing exercises and transitions to maintain a calming atmosphere.
