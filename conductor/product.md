# Product Definition

## Vision
YAKAP is a comprehensive mental health support platform designed to bridge the gap between daily self-care and professional clinical treatment. It aims to provide a safe, empathetic, and efficient ecosystem for patients, mental health professionals, and administrators.

## Core Problem
YAKAP addresses three critical issues in the mental health landscape:
1.  **Access:** Limited availability of affordable and timely mental health resources.
2.  **Continuity of Care:** The disconnection between a patient's daily self-care routine (like mood tracking) and their professional clinical sessions.
3.  **Administrative Burden:** The high workload for professionals in managing patient data and appointments.

## Target Audience
The platform serves three distinct user roles:
*   **Patients/Users:** Individuals seeking support for mental well-being, mood tracking, and self-care.
*   **Mental Health Professionals:** Psychiatrists, Psychologists, and Psychometricians providing care, managing patients, and conducting consultations.
*   **Admins:** Platform managers responsible for moderation, user verification, and system analytics.

## Key Features

### 1. Authentication & Onboarding
*   **Role-Based Access:** Secure sign-up and login flows tailored for Users, Professionals, and Admins.
*   **Onboarding:** A gentle, privacy-focused introduction to YAKAP’s mission and features.
*   **Splash Screen:** A welcoming initial load experience.

### 2. Patient/User Experience
*   **Dashboard:** A central hub for daily mood tracking (using emojis and notes) and self-care journaling.
*   **Self-Assessment:** Tools and quizzes to gauge stress and anxiety levels.
*   **Professional Connection:** Seamless appointment booking and secure real-time chat with mental health professionals.
*   **Wellness Tools:** Guided breathing and grounding exercises with soothing animations.
*   **Community:** Peer support forums for shared experiences.
*   **Engagement:** Smart notifications for daily check-ins, reminders, and well-being tips.

### 3. Professional Experience
*   **Patient Management:** Comprehensive lists and profiles for managing patient care.
*   **Scheduling:** Integrated calendar for appointment management.
*   **Communication:** Secure, direct messaging channel with patients.
*   **Clinical Notes:** Encrypted session note-taking with secure local storage and backend synchronization.

### 4. Admin Experience
*   **User Management:** Tools for verifying professional credentials and moderating user accounts.
*   **Content Management:** System to update self-care tips, exercises, and platform content.
*   **Analytics:** Dashboard for monitoring user engagement metrics and overall system health.
